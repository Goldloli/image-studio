The text prompts T01–T03 are frozen from the active OpenSpec test plan.

T04 uses the official MOSS layout contract documented at:

- Repository: `https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-Image`
- Pinned revision: `2b46b1e0e13b5aaec253879e0d006a42564b8b27`
- Document: `USERGUIDE.md`, layout prompt section

- Box order: `[y_min, x_min, y_max, x_max]` (`yxyx`)

The T04 content and coordinates are original to this project. They describe a
1024×1024 weekend-market poster with title, subtitle, and left/right objects.
